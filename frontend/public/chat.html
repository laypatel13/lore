Why did we switch from Webpack to Turbopack in 2023?
LORE cognee.recall() · 4 sources
The migration was driven by persistent build time complaints documented across 47 issues throughout 2022–2023. The tipping point was a community benchmark in issue #42847 showing cold starts exceeding 90 seconds on large enterprise repos.

PR #51203 by @sokra introduced the Turbopack integration, with benchmarks confirming 10x faster cold starts and 700ms HMR on a 5,000-module project. The architectural decision was formalized in an RFC authored by @timneutkens on September 14, 2023, which also documented 3 earlier failed migration attempts dating back to 2021.
